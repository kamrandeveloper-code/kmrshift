## Planned section briefs

### Systems We Migrate
Mono-styled grid or ticker of source stacks (Access, FoxPro, VB6, Delphi,
COBOL, Lotus Notes, Excel macros) mapped to targets (React, PostgreSQL, Cloud).
Lets visitors instantly confirm their stack is covered.

### Work / Case Studies
2–3 before → after transformations. Each: legacy screenshot vs. modern app
(reuse the hero `InterfaceFrame` Phase 01 → 02 style), industry, old stack
(e.g. "Access 2003, 40 users") and one hard result metric.

### Testimonial
Single large quote from a client decision-maker (ops manager / CFO) on Paper.

### About
Who is behind KMR Shift, why legacy systems, short founder note.

### FAQ / Risk
Accordion with sharp hairline rules answering buyer fears: migration failure,
staff retraining, data loss, cost.
